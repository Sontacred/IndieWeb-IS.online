# IndieWeb_IS.online
Personnal website for creation and sharing stuff that I think about or I am interested in.

## Reason

I'm a bit fanof webdesign and website conception. I also enjoy seeing how people express themselve through a website with sometime original and confusing ideas that makes the whole experience unique everytime.

I also think that a website is the best platform to share stuff the way you like while social medias or bigger platforms all seems to lack some features or make it hard to be seen through algorithm and other stuff.

Also my mind have been evolving a lot and the perception of my online identity and style has changed a lot. I made A LOT of websites that I never used and since I've started to actually post on social medias and get a better idea of my personal aesthetic, I may start from scratch and hopefully this one will be THE ONE.


## Content 

I will share everithing I drew since 2025 and share some thoughts, achievements and process in a blog page. 
I willing to not only stick to drawing but also make other type of art like music, 3D rendering, visual design or video games.

I may make a commission page if I'm able to manage my time and create a routine good enough to leave me consistent time to market my creations.

## About me

I just a guy willing to create stuff and enjoy things in this life.
